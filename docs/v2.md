# Dead Keys v2 list

The build is frozen for Kiroween judging (code freeze at `1f1d259`, Nov 13 2026). Feedback and known limits collected after the freeze land here. Nothing on this list is built yet.

## From player feedback

| # | Item | Source | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Make keyboard navigation easier to find | Kiro Discord, Nov 14 2026 | Arrow keys, Enter and Escape already work on the menus and Lessons, and the menu footer says so, but a player didn't find it. Make the hint more visible |
| 2 | Letter hotkeys on menus (P for Play, L for Lessons, and so on), with the letter highlighted | Kiro Discord, Nov 14 2026 | WCAG 2.1.4 Character Key Shortcuts: menus only, never during play, and a Settings switch to turn them off |
| 3 | Quick sound toggle on the menu and the play screen (for example M to mute) | Kiro Discord, Nov 14 2026 | Sound already lives in Settings (Off, Typewriter, Arcade), off by default |
