# Requirements: Block 8a, lessons (letters)

**Spec:** `lessons` (PRD G5 lesson rules, G7 lesson ladder, G11 screens). **Rigor:** Working for layout, Full for anything that records a lesson or decides a pass.
**Status:** Draft by Claude, Nov 12. Waiting for Shara's approval. Mock approved Nov 12 before this spec was written.
**Sources:** `mock/lessons.html` (locked Nov 12: the Lesson screen, the Lessons menu, the Lesson done card) and `mock/index.html` screen 2 (the main menu's "Recommended next lesson" panel) are the spec for how lessons look. The mock-to-build map and the copy table in `design.md` are binding. Research: `claude/dead-keys-curriculum-research.md` in the project (lesson anatomy, sources 9 to 11). Plan and guardrails: `claude/dead-keys-block-8-plan.md`.

## Introduction

Lessons teach the keyboard one row at a time. Each lesson is a calm typing line, not a game: no falling words, no lives, no timer. A lesson passes on accuracy, never speed. This half of Block 8 (8a) builds Lessons 0 to 6, the letters. Block 8b adds Lessons 7 to 13 (capitals, punctuation, numbers, symbols, real-world strings, common pairs).

## Scope

**In:** the lesson ladder data for 0 to 13 (7 to 13 shown as "later"); Lessons 0 to 6 playable; the four-part lesson; test out for Lessons 1 to 6; the pass rule; recording a lesson and a pass; the Lessons menu; the Lesson screen; the Lesson done card; finger colors on the lesson keyboard; the main menu's recommended lesson panel; the first-visit nudge; the dip notice; "How we count" lesson rules; the Block 7 carry-ins (Req 10).
**Out (STUB, never half-built):** Lessons 7 to 13 (Block 8b), the "what's this?" glossary (8b), the Finger colors switch in Settings (stays "Not built yet" until 8b; colors are always on in Lessons 0 to 6, as PRD G7 says), drills (Block 9), Spanish lessons (Block 15).

## Requirement 1: The ladder

1. `src/lessons/ladder.js` SHALL define Lessons 0 to 13 exactly as PRD G7 lists them (number, name, new keys), each with an id `L00` to `L13`, a group (`home`, `top`, `bottom`, `beyond`), and `built: true` for 0 to 6, `false` for 7 to 13.
2. A lesson's learned keys SHALL be its new keys plus every earlier lesson's new keys. Space is a learned key in every lesson from Lesson 1 on.
3. Every built lesson SHALL be open: a player may start or test out of any of Lessons 0 to 6 in any order. Lessons 7 to 13 show "Later" and cannot be started.

## Requirement 2: What a lesson is

1. Lessons 1 to 6 SHALL have four parts in order: Warm-up, New keys, Mix, Check (research: lesson anatomy). Lesson 0 has its own four parts (Req 6).
2. The lines for every part SHALL be generated by `src/lessons/generate.js` from the lesson's keys with the seeded RNG (`src/engine/rng.js`), so the same seed gives the same lesson and a replay gets a new seed. The generator is pure.
3. Every character in every line SHALL be one of the lesson's learned keys. Lines are lowercase. No line is longer than 28 characters.
4. Warm-up: 1 line of patterns from keys learned before this lesson (Lesson 1 warms up on `f` and `j`). New keys: 2 lines of patterns on the new keys, each pattern anchored on a home key. Mix: 1 line of patterns mixing new and old keys, then 1 line of real words. Check: 2 lines of real words and patterns, at least 50 characters together.
5. Real words SHALL come from `packs/lessonWords.json` for Lessons 1 to 3 (the pack has almost no words that use only those keys) and from the general pack's words for Lessons 4 to 6, keeping only words whose letters are all learned keys. Every word in `lessonWords.json` SHALL pass the same checks as the general pack (SCOWL en_US, not on the blocklist) and use only its lesson's learned keys.

## Requirement 3: Typing a line

1. `src/lessons/line.js` SHALL be a pure typing-line engine: the line, how much is typed, the wrong key if any, and the keystrokes.
2. The right key moves on. A wrong key never moves on: it is kept as the line's wrong key and counted as a miss against the expected key (the same strict-but-clear rule as Play). Backspace clears the wrong key and nothing else. A space is a real key in lessons (unlike Play) and counts like any other.
3. When a line is finished the next line starts. When the last line of a part is finished the next part starts. When the Check's last line is finished the lesson is done.
4. Timing SHALL count only the gaps between keystrokes, each gap capped at 5 seconds, and nothing while paused, so walking away does not lower the speed.

## Requirement 4: Passing, testing out and recording

1. A lesson (1 to 6) SHALL pass when the Check part's accuracy is 95% or more, at any speed. Lesson 0 passes when it is finished.
2. Test out SHALL be the Check part only, generated for that lesson. It passes on the same rule.
3. A finished lesson SHALL be recorded as one RoundResult (mode `lesson`, or `testout` for a test out; `lessonId` set; `cleared` and `wave` 0; `drillSource` null; `continued` false) covering every keystroke of every part, so the heat map learns from the whole lesson.
4. `record()` SHALL take an optional pass (`{ lessonId, how }`) and add it to `lessonsPassed` before milestones are computed, once per lesson (a second pass of the same lesson adds nothing; a test out after a pass adds nothing). The lesson-passed milestone then comes from the existing milestone code.
5. Leaving a lesson before it is done SHALL record nothing.

## Requirement 5: The Lessons menu

1. The main menu's Lessons item SHALL open the Lessons menu (no longer "Not built yet").
2. The Lessons menu SHALL show every lesson in one list grouped by row, each with its number, name, new keys and state: Passed, Tested out, Next, Open or Later. Each group shows how many of its built lessons are passed.
3. The learned-keys keyboard SHALL light the keys of every passed lesson and outline the next lesson's new keys.
4. The card SHALL show the selected lesson: number, name, new keys, the four parts, Start lesson and Test out (Lesson 0 has no Test out; Later lessons show "Not built yet" and no buttons).
5. Up and Down move the selection; Enter starts the selected lesson; T tests out of it; Escape goes back. The list starts on the recommended lesson.

## Requirement 6: Lesson 0, Meet your keyboard

1. Part 1, Your keyboard: a picture of the US keyboard and the question "Does your keyboard look like this?" with Yes and No. No shows the copy-table line and the lesson goes on.
2. Part 2, Home keys; Part 3, Fingers; Part 4, Eyes up: each a coach line from the copy table and one short typing line on `f`, `j`, `d`, `k` as the copy table gives.
3. Finishing Part 4 passes Lesson 0.

## Requirement 7: The Lesson screen and Lesson done

1. The Lesson screen SHALL match mock screen C per the map: the four steps, the coach line, the paper typing line, the wrong-key hint, live accuracy and keys typed, the finger-colored keyboard with learned keys bright, other keys dimmed and the next key filled, the legend, and the footer.
2. Escape SHALL open a pause dialog: Resume, or Leave lesson (Req 4.5). Main menu in the header asks the same before leaving.
3. When the lesson is done the Lesson done card SHALL match mock screen E per the map: Passed or Not yet, Check accuracy against 95%, speed with "any speed is fine", the two most-missed keys in this lesson (or the copy-table line when there were none), the dip notice when Req 9 says so, and the buttons. Enter takes the first button; Escape goes to the Lessons menu.
4. Lesson screens use the desk look: no scanlines, the paper colors in `design.md`. Play screens keep the arcade look.
5. A screen reader SHALL hear each new line read out, a wrong key as the copy-table line, and the Lesson done result. Nothing relies on color alone: the next key is filled, the wrong key is struck through, the waiting letter is underlined.

## Requirement 8: Recommended next lesson

1. `src/lessons/recommend.js` SHALL pick, among built lessons not yet passed, the earliest that has one of the player's top 3 weak keys (heat map bands, enough data only); otherwise the earliest not yet passed; otherwise none.
2. A player with no lessons passed and no rounds SHALL get Lesson 0 with the first-visit line ("New to typing? Start here.").
3. The main menu's right column SHALL show the recommended lesson in place of the "Not built yet" panel, per the map for mock screen 2. When every built lesson is passed it shows the copy-table line.

## Requirement 9: The dip notice

1. The Lesson done card SHALL show the dip line when this is the player's second or third lesson round ever and its WPM is more than 15% below their first lesson round's WPM (PRD G5).
2. "How we count" SHALL state the pass rule, test out, timing (Req 3.4) and the dip rule in the copy table's words.

## Requirement 10: Block 7 carry-ins

1. Results: the two landmarks both named "Accuracy" get distinct names; the hidden h1 on play screens and the results h1 sit inside `<main>`. Both from the copy table.
2. Results: a first round that earns several WPM bands at once shows only the highest band.
3. "Paused." and "Out of lives." are each spoken once.
4. Check that `npm run deploy:preview` uses the project's pinned Vercel CLI (`node_modules/.bin/vercel`), not a global one, and report what it finds. Do not upgrade anything.

2. e2e (keyboard only): open Lessons, start Lesson 1, type a whole lesson with one wrong key fixed by Backspace and one by the right key, see Passed, and see Lesson 1 passed on the menu and the main menu panel move on; test out of Lesson 2 with misses and see Not yet; leave a lesson and see nothing recorded; Lesson 0 end to end.
3. axe on the Lessons menu, the Lesson screen (normal and wrong-key), the pause dialog, Lesson done (passed and not yet), and the main menu with the new panel. 0 serious or critical.
4. No page scrolls at 1440x900, 1366x768 and 1280x720 on any lesson screen.
5. Screenshot pairs against `docs/screens/mock-lesson.png`, `mock-lessons.png`, `mock-lesson-done.png` and the main menu mock, at 1440x900 and 1366x768.
